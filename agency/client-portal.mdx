Every agency has fielded the request: *"Can you just update the team page real quick?"* The client portal is designed to make that question disappear. When you give a client their own Synclify login, they can trigger syncs, check status, and review results entirely on their own — without ever touching Webflow, your agency account, or your workflow. You stay in control of the configuration; they gain the autonomy to act on it.

## What clients can do in the portal

Once a client logs in, they have access to a focused view of their site's sync activity. From the client portal, a client can:

- View the current sync status for their site
- Trigger a manual sync using the **Sync Now** button
- View the full sync log and inspect record-level results
- Download error reports to review or share with their own team

## What clients cannot do

The client portal is intentionally scoped. Clients have read-and-trigger access only — they cannot make configuration changes or see anything outside their own site:

- They **cannot** view or access other clients' sites
- They **cannot** change field mappings or sync configurations
- They **cannot** add or remove data sources
- They **cannot** access your Webflow account, API credentials, or agency settings

## Creating a client login

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the client's site in your dashboard">
    In the [agency dashboard](/agency/dashboard), click the name of the site you want to grant access to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Client Access settings">
    Click **Settings → Client Access** in the left-hand navigation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite the client">
    Click **Invite Client** to open the invitation form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the client's email">
    Type the client's email address and click **Send Invite**. Synclify sends an invitation email immediately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Client creates their password">
    The client receives an email containing a secure link. They click it, set a password, and their account is created.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Client logs in to their portal">
    Once they log in, they see only the sync panel for their site — nothing else is visible.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Managing client access

You can revoke a client's access at any time. Navigate to **Settings → Client Access** for the relevant site and click **Revoke** next to the client's email. Their login is immediately disabled and they can no longer access the portal.

If a client later needs access restored, you can re-invite them following the same steps above.

<Note>
  Each client portal login is scoped to a single site. If a client has multiple sites with you, invite them separately for each site so they only ever see what's relevant to them.
</Note>

To add a new client site before setting up access, see [Managing Sites](/agency/managing-sites).

<Tip>
  Share the client portal link during onboarding so clients can self-serve sync triggers without waiting on you. Include a short Loom video walkthrough for non-technical clients — a 90-second demo of the Sync Now button and the log view is usually all it takes.
</Tip>
