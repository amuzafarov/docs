Connecting your Webflow site to Synclify authorizes the app to read your CMS Collections and write synced data into them. Once connected, Synclify automatically discovers every CMS Collection on your site — no manual configuration required. From there, you can map source columns from Google Sheets, Airtable, or Notion to your Collection fields and start syncing in minutes.

## Requirements

Before connecting, make sure you have the following:

- A Webflow account with at least one site on the **CMS**, **Business**, or **Enterprise** plan
- At least one CMS Collection on the site you want to sync
- **Admin** or **Editor** access to the Webflow site

<Note>
  Synclify is not compatible with Webflow's Basic or Starter plans, as those plans do not include CMS Collections.
</Note>

## Connecting Your Site

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Sites panel">
    In Synclify, navigate to **Sites** in the left sidebar and click **Add Site**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the Webflow authorization">
    Click **Connect Webflow**. You will be redirected to Webflow's authorization page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your workspace and site">
    Choose the Webflow workspace and the specific site you want to authorize from the dropdown menus on Webflow's authorization page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize access">
    Click **Authorize**. Webflow will redirect you back to Synclify automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Synclify discovers your Collections">
    Synclify scans your site and automatically detects all CMS Collections. No manual import or setup is needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm your site is listed">
    Your site now appears in the **Sites** list, with all detected Collections nested beneath it and ready to configure.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Managing Multiple Sites

If you manage several Webflow sites, Synclify's agency plan supports connecting **15 or more sites** from a single account. You can add each site individually by repeating the steps above. For guidance on organizing and switching between multiple connected sites, see the [Managing Sites](/agency/managing-sites) page.

## Disconnecting a Site

To remove a connected site, go to **Sites**, select the site name, open **Settings**, and click **Disconnect**. Disconnecting immediately removes the site from Synclify and stops all active syncs associated with it.

<Warning>
  Disconnecting a site removes **all sync configurations** for that site from Synclify. This action cannot be undone. Your existing Webflow CMS content is completely unaffected — no items are deleted from Webflow when you disconnect.
</Warning>

<Note>
  Synclify only ever writes to Webflow CMS Collections. It never reads or modifies your Webflow site design, pages, or settings.
</Note>
