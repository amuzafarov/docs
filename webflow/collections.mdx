Webflow CMS Collections are the containers that hold your structured content — products, blog posts, team members, and so on. Synclify treats each Collection as a sync target: it reads rows from your connected source and writes them into the Collection as individual items. Understanding how Synclify creates, updates, and matches those items will help you keep your Webflow content accurate and predictable.

## How Rows Become Collection Items

Every row in your source maps to exactly one CMS Collection item in Webflow:

- **New rows** — when Synclify finds a source row with a slug that does not yet exist in the Collection, it creates a new CMS item.
- **Updated rows** — when a source row's slug matches an existing CMS item, Synclify updates that item with the latest values from the source.
- **Deleted rows** — if a row is removed from your source, Synclify does **not** automatically delete the corresponding CMS item from Webflow. See the note at the bottom of this page for details.

## Item Matching (Slug)

Synclify uses the **Slug** field as the unique identifier to match source rows to existing CMS items. Before writing any data, Synclify checks whether a CMS item with that slug already exists in the Collection:

- **Slug exists** → the item is updated with the current source data.
- **Slug does not exist** → a new item is created.

This means your slug values must be stable and unique across rows. If a slug changes between syncs, Synclify will create a duplicate item rather than updating the original. To keep slugs consistent, map a reliable unique identifier — such as a record ID, SKU, or handle — from your source to the Slug field in your [field mapping](/webflow/field-mapping) configuration.

## Published vs. Draft

By default, every item Synclify creates or updates is saved as a **Draft** in Webflow. You can change this behavior per Collection.

To update the default publish status, go to your Collection's settings in Synclify and select **Default publish status**. The available options are:

- **Draft** *(default)* — items are staged in Webflow and must be published manually before they appear on your live site.
- **Published** — items go live on your Webflow site immediately after each sync completes.

<Tip>
  Use Draft mode when you want to review synced content — for example, to check formatting or images — before it goes live on your Webflow site.
</Tip>

## Multi-Collection Syncs

A single Webflow site can have many CMS Collections, and Synclify supports syncing all of them independently. You can connect a different source to each Collection, and each Collection maintains its own field mapping configuration.

For example, you might connect a Google Sheet to your **Products** Collection and an Airtable base to your **Blog Posts** Collection — all within the same Webflow site. Each Collection syncs on its own schedule and uses its own field mappings, with no overlap or interference between them.

<Note>
  Synclify does not delete CMS items when source rows are removed. If you need to remove a CMS item, delete it directly in the **Webflow CMS editor** or via the **Webflow CMS API**. This behavior is intentional and protects against accidental content loss from unintended source edits.
</Note>
