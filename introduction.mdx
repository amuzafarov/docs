Synclify is a no-code data sync platform built specifically for Webflow. It reads content from Google Sheets, Airtable, and Notion and writes it directly into your Webflow CMS Collections on a schedule you control — no custom code, no brittle Zapier chains, and no manual copy-pasting. Connect a source once, map your fields visually, and let Synclify keep your CMS content in sync automatically from that point forward.

## Who is Synclify for?

Synclify is designed first and foremost for **Webflow agencies** that manage multiple client sites and need a reliable, scalable way to keep CMS content current without touching each site manually. If you're juggling ten, fifteen, or more Webflow projects — each with its own editorial spreadsheet or database — Synclify gives you a single dashboard to oversee every sync and every client in one place.

**Synclify is a great fit if you:**

- Run a Webflow agency or freelance practice managing more than one client site
- Have clients who maintain their own content in Google Sheets, Airtable, or Notion and want those changes reflected on their live site automatically
- Want to offer clients a self-service portal to trigger syncs and view logs without granting them full Webflow access
- Need structured, auditable sync history with downloadable error reports

## Supported sources

Synclify currently supports three data sources. Each source is authenticated once per workspace and can power syncs across multiple Webflow Collections.

- **Google Sheets** — Connect any spreadsheet in your Google Drive. Synclify reads the first row as your column headers and maps each subsequent row to a CMS Collection item.
- **Airtable** — Link any Airtable base and choose the specific table you want to sync. Field types — including attachments, selects, and linked records — are mapped intelligently to their Webflow equivalents.
- **Notion** — Use a Notion database as your content source. Synclify reads Notion database properties and maps them to Webflow CMS fields, keeping your Notion workspace as the single source of truth.

## Supported Webflow plans

Synclify works with any Webflow site plan that includes CMS functionality. This covers the **CMS**, **Business**, and **Enterprise** plans. Synclify is not compatible with the Basic or Starter plans, which do not include Webflow CMS Collections.

If you are unsure which plan your client site is on, you can check under **Site Settings → Hosting** in the Webflow Designer.
