Find answers to the most common questions about Synclify below. If your question isn't covered here, join the community via the waitlist at [synclify.cloud](https://synclify.cloud) or reach out through the support link in your dashboard — the team is happy to help.

<AccordionGroup>

  <Accordion title="When is Synclify launching?">
    Synclify is targeting a launch on the **Webflow App Marketplace in 2026**. Everyone on the waitlist receives early access before the public launch, so you can start syncing and provide feedback ahead of the general release. Join the waitlist at [synclify.cloud](https://synclify.cloud).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Which Webflow plans does Synclify support?">
    Synclify works with any Webflow plan that includes CMS Collections: the **CMS, Business, and Enterprise** plans. It does not work with the Basic or Starter plans, which do not include CMS Collections. If you are unsure which plan you are on, check your Webflow workspace settings.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Which data sources does Synclify support?">
    Synclify supports **Google Sheets, Airtable, and Notion** at launch — all three are available on day one. Additional source integrations may be added in future releases based on community feedback.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does Synclify replace Whalesync or CMS Bridge?">
    Yes. Synclify covers the same core use case — syncing external data into Webflow CMS — and supports the same primary sources: Google Sheets, Airtable, and Notion. It also adds an **agency-first multi-client dashboard**, so you can manage syncs across multiple Webflow sites from a single place, something neither Whalesync nor CMS Bridge was designed to do.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is my data safe?">
    Yes. Synclify is built with several layers of protection:

    - **OAuth tokens** are encrypted at rest using **AES-256-GCM**
    - **All data in transit** is protected by **TLS 1.3**
    - **Your source data** is read only at sync time and written directly to Webflow — it is never stored in Synclify's systems

    Your spreadsheets and databases remain your own. See the [Security](security) page for a full breakdown.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does Synclify delete CMS items when I delete source rows?">
    No. Synclify only **creates and updates** CMS items — it never deletes them automatically. If you remove a row from Google Sheets, a record from Airtable, or an entry from Notion, the corresponding Webflow CMS item remains in place. To remove a CMS item, delete it directly in Webflow.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I manage multiple client Webflow sites?">
    Yes. The **agency plan** lets you manage 15 or more Webflow sites from a single Synclify dashboard. Each site gets its own sync configuration, and you can create a dedicated client portal for each one so clients can monitor their own syncs without accessing your full dashboard.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can my clients trigger syncs themselves?">
    Yes. Invite clients to their site's **client portal** and they can trigger manual syncs and view sync logs on their own — without needing Webflow credentials or access to your agency account. Client portal logins are scoped to a single site and cannot see any other client's data.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I sync only some rows from my spreadsheet?">
    Use your source tool's built-in filtering before connecting to Synclify:

    - **Google Sheets** — create a filtered view or use a dedicated sheet that contains only the rows you want to sync
    - **Airtable** — create a filtered view in your base and select that view when configuring the connection in Synclify
    - **Notion** — apply filters to your database view before connecting it to Synclify

    Synclify reads whichever view or sheet you point it at, so controlling what it syncs is as simple as controlling what appears in that view.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens if a sync fails?">
    Synclify handles failures at the row level. If an individual row or record fails to sync — for example, due to a missing required field or a data type mismatch — that row is **logged and skipped** while the rest of the sync continues. You can view all errors in the **sync log** inside your dashboard and download a CSV error report to identify and fix any issues before re-running the sync.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I get support?">
    Use the **support link** in your Synclify dashboard to contact the team directly. You can also join the early access community by signing up for the waitlist at [synclify.cloud](https://synclify.cloud) — waitlist members get access to community channels where you can ask questions, share feedback, and connect with other agencies using Synclify.
  </Accordion>
