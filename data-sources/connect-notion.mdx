<Note>
  **Coming soon.** Notion is not yet available as a data source. Airtable is the supported source today — see [Connect Airtable](/data-sources/connect-airtable).
</Note>

We're building Notion support so you can use a Notion database as the content source for a Webflow CMS collection — the same guided setup, field mapping, and realtime sync you get with Airtable.

## What to expect

When it ships, connecting Notion will work like every other source:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Authorize via OAuth">
    Connect your Notion workspace from **Data Sources** and choose which databases to share.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a database">
    Select the Notion database to sync. Its properties become mappable fields.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Map and activate">
    [Map fields](/connections/field-mapping), choose a [sync direction](/how-synclify-works#sync-direction), and let [guided setup](/connections/guided-setup) reconcile existing records.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## In the meantime

If your content lives in Notion today, the fastest path to Webflow is to mirror it into an Airtable table and [connect that](/data-sources/connect-airtable).

Want Notion sooner? Let us know at [help@synclify.cloud](mailto:help@synclify.cloud) — demand shapes the roadmap.
