## Get up and running in four steps

Not sure where to begin? Follow this high-level path to complete your first sync, then explore the rest of the docs at your own pace.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect Webflow">
    Authorize Synclify with your Webflow account using a single OAuth click. Synclify automatically detects every CMS Collection across all your sites — no manual configuration needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick your data source">
    Choose Google Sheets, Airtable, or Notion as your content source and authenticate once. Synclify reads the source structure and surfaces your columns or properties for mapping.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Map your fields">
    Use the visual drag-and-drop field mapper to connect source columns to Webflow CMS fields. Preview real data inline before you save, so you know exactly what will sync.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sync automatically">
    Set a schedule — hourly, daily, weekly, or a custom interval — or trigger a sync manually at any time from the dashboard or client portal. Synclify handles the rest.
  </Step>
</Steps>
