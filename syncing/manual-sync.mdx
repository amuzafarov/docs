Manual syncs let you push data to Webflow on demand, outside of your normal schedule. They're useful whenever you need changes reflected in your CMS right away — for example, after an urgent data update, before a publishing deadline, or when testing a new field mapping and you want to verify the results immediately.

## Triggering from the dashboard

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Collection in your dashboard">
    Go to your site's Collection in the Synclify dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the sync">
    Click **Sync Now**. The sync starts immediately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the sync to complete">
    The **Sync Now** button shows a spinner while the sync is running. Do not navigate away — the page updates automatically when the sync finishes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the summary">
    When complete, a summary appears showing the number of items created, updated, skipped, and any errors encountered.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Inspect the full log">
    Click **View Log** to open the record-level detail and see exactly what happened to each row. See [Sync Logs and Errors](/syncing/logs-and-errors) for a full breakdown of statuses and error reports.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Triggering from the client portal

Client portal users can also trigger manual syncs without needing direct access to Webflow or the main Synclify dashboard. In the client portal, click **Sync Now** on the relevant Collection. The sync starts immediately and displays the same created / updated / skipped / error summary once it finishes. This makes it easy to give clients or content managers the ability to refresh their own CMS data on demand.

## What happens during a sync

Every sync — manual or scheduled — follows the same process:

1. **Synclify reads your source data.** It fetches the latest rows from your connected Google Sheets spreadsheet, Airtable base, or Notion database.
2. **Each row is matched or created.** Synclify compares each row to existing Webflow CMS items using the Slug field. If a match is found, the item is updated. If no match exists, a new item is created.
3. **Items are written to Webflow CMS.** Fields are mapped and written to the Collection according to your [field mapping configuration](/webflow/field-mapping).
4. **The sync log is updated.** Every row receives a status — Created, Updated, Skipped, or Error — and the log is saved for your review.

<Note>
  Manual syncs do not affect your schedule. A scheduled sync will still run at its configured time regardless of when you last triggered a manual sync.
</Note>

<Warning>
  Running two syncs simultaneously on the same Collection is not supported. If you trigger a manual sync while a scheduled one is already running, the manual sync will queue and start automatically after the current one finishes.
</Warning>
