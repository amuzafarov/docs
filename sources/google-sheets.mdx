Connecting Google Sheets to Synclify lets you turn any spreadsheet into a live Webflow CMS Collection. Once connected, Synclify reads your rows at each scheduled or manual sync and pushes them as CMS items — no exports, no copy-pasting, and no manual updates required. This is ideal for managing product listings, team directories, blog post metadata, or any structured content you already maintain in a sheet.

## Before you begin

Make sure the following are in place before you connect your spreadsheet:

- **Header row:** Row 1 of your sheet must contain column names. Synclify uses these as field labels in the visual field mapper.
- **Account access:** The sheet must be accessible by the Google account you'll authenticate with — either as the owner or with at least Viewer-level sharing.
- **Webflow plan:** Your Webflow site must be on a CMS, Business, or Enterprise plan to use CMS Collections.

## Connect Google Sheets

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Sources">
    Go to **Sources** in your Synclify dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a new source">
    Click **Add Source → Google Sheets**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in with Google">
    Sign in with your Google account when prompted. Synclify requests **read-only** access to your spreadsheets — it cannot edit or delete your data.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your spreadsheet and sheet">
    Choose the spreadsheet you want to sync, then select the specific sheet (tab) within that spreadsheet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview your data">
    Synclify displays a preview of your column headers and a sample of your rows. Confirm that the data looks correct before continuing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the source">
    Click **Save Source**. Your Google Sheet is now registered as a source and is ready to be mapped to a Webflow CMS Collection.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Column requirements

Synclify reads your sheet starting from row 1, which must contain your column headers. Keep the following in mind when structuring your spreadsheet:

- **Header row is required.** Row 1 must contain column names. Synclify uses these names as field identifiers in the mapper.
- **Empty columns are skipped.** Any column without a header in row 1 is ignored during sync.
- **Supported data types:** Synclify recognises the following values in your cells:
  - **Text** — any string value
  - **Number** — integers and decimals
  - **URL** — full URLs beginning with `http://` or `https://`
  - **Image URL** — a URL pointing directly to an image file
  - **Boolean** — cells containing exactly `TRUE` or `FALSE` (case-insensitive)

## Supported field types

Use the table below to plan how your Google Sheets columns map to Webflow CMS field types in the visual field mapper.

| Google Sheets column type | Compatible Webflow field types |
|---------------------------|-------------------------------|
| Text column               | Plain Text, Rich Text          |
| Number column             | Number                         |
| URL column                | Link, Image (for image URLs)   |
| TRUE/FALSE column         | Switch (boolean)               |

<Note>
  Synclify requests **read-only** access to your Google Sheets. It never modifies, overwrites, or deletes anything in your spreadsheet.
</Note>

## Reconnecting or switching sheets

If you need to authenticate with a different Google account, point the source at a new spreadsheet, or change the active sheet tab, go to **Sources**, find your existing Google Sheets source, and click **Edit**. From there you can re-authorize your Google account or select a different spreadsheet and tab. Changing the sheet resets any unsaved field mappings, so make sure your mapping is saved before switching.

<Tip>
  Name your sheet tabs clearly — for example, **Products**, **Blog Posts**, or **Team Members** — so they're easy to identify when selecting a sheet in Synclify.
</Tip>
