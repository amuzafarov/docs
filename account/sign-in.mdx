Synclify accounts are per person. Your workspace is what holds connections, data sources, and billing.

## Creating an account

Registration starts with an **email address only** — no password yet.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter your email">
    **Send verification link.** _"Start by verifying your email address."_
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your inbox">
    _"Your profile and password are created only after you verify your address."_ A single-use verification link is on its way.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finish creating your account">
    The link opens **Finish creating your account** — _"Your email is verified. Choose your name and password to create your workspace."_ Password minimum is **8 characters**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can also register with **Google**.

<Note>
  Signing in with Google is unrelated to the Google Sheets data source. One is how you get into Synclify; the other is a place Synclify reads content from.
</Note>

## Signing in

Three routes, all from the same page:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Password">
    Email and password. The **Sign in** button submits.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Email link">
    **Email me a sign-in link** sends a one-time link — _"a sign-in link is on its way. It works once and expires in 15 minutes."_ Resending is rate-limited with a short countdown.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Google">
    OAuth, same account as registration.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

### Forgot your password

The emailed sign-in link **is** the password-recovery path — there is no separate reset flow. Use **Forgot?** and follow the link, which offers an optional set-a-new-password field alongside signing in.

<Note>
  The confirmation you get is the same whether or not the address has an account. That is deliberate: it stops the page being used to discover who has a Synclify account.
</Note>

### Why the link isn't consumed when it loads

Corporate mail scanners follow links in email before you ever click them. If loading the page consumed the link, your sign-in would be spent by a security filter.

So the page **waits**. It shows **Finish signing in** — _"You followed a sign-in link. Continue to open your workspace."_ — and does nothing until you press the button.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="This sign-in link doesn't work anymore">
    _"It may have expired or already been used. Sign-in links work once and expire after 15 minutes."_ Go back to sign-in and request a fresh one.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="This verification link doesn't work anymore">
    The registration equivalent. _"Start registration again to receive a fresh link."_
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Email sign-in is not available yet">
    Link sign-in isn't enabled for this account. _"Sign in with your password instead."_
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Too many requests">
    _"Wait a few minutes before requesting another link."_
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Two-factor challenge

If [two-factor authentication](/account/workspace-security#two-factor-authentication) is on, sign-in adds a code step — for password, email link, and Google alike.

Enter the current code from your authenticator app, or an unused recovery code. If it's rejected: _"That code didn't work — Enter the current code from your authenticator app or a recovery code."_

An attempt that sits too long expires: _"Sign-in attempt expired — Enter your email and password again."_ Start over; nothing is wrong with your account.

## Accepting an invite

An invite link opens `/invite`, which works whether or not you're signed in. Enter your name and a password (at least 8 characters, confirmed). If the address already has Synclify credentials, those are kept rather than replaced.

See [Team](/account/team) for how invites are issued and revoked.

## Confirm your email

Until your address is verified, an amber banner sits in the app: _"Confirm your email address to secure your account."_ **Resend verification email** sends another, once per page load.

## Sessions

Your session is a **cookie**, protected against cross-site request forgery. No token is held in browser storage where a script could read it.

Signing out doesn't just forget who you are — it resets the app. Every store returns to its initial state, the cached data is dropped, and Synclify's local storage is cleared, including saved connection-wizard drafts. Your device's **theme** preference is kept.

<Note>
  The same reset happens when you [switch workspaces](/account/team#switching-workspaces). The next account to use the tab sees nothing belonging to the previous one.
</Note>

## Onboarding

New accounts land in onboarding, which walks you through connecting a source, mapping fields, and previewing before the first sync. Webflow must be authorized before you can build anything — see [Connect Webflow](/data-sources/connect-webflow).

Notion and Google Sheets appear as **Coming soon** and can't be selected yet.
